## Short version — `#releases` / `#changelog`

> **DayZ Inventory 1.8.0 is out — 23 Minecraft versions, one download away.**
>
> The mod now ships for **everything from 1.20.1 to 26.3**, on **Fabric, NeoForge and Forge** — 53
> files, all built from one source tree. Every version from 1.20.6 onward has Fabric *and* NeoForge;
> Forge covers 1.20.6 through 1.21.11, and Fabric covers the 1.20.1–1.20.5 stretch on its own.
>
> **Two real bugs fixed along the way:**
>
> - On **1.20.1–1.20.4** the chest and barrel hooks were pointing at a method that does not exist on
>   those versions, so chests and barrels quietly fell back to their vanilla screens instead of
>   opening in the DayZ UI. That works now.
> - The JEI / REI / EMI and Curios buttons could appear without doing anything, and Curios does not
>   exist on Fabric at all. Both behave properly now.
>
> The Forge files were also missing from the GitHub release pages of every past version — they went
> to Modrinth and CurseForge but never got attached to the release. Fixed here.
>
> Downloads: https://modrinth.com/mod/dayz-inventory/versions
> Changelog: https://github.com/aacanadaa/DayZ-Inventory/releases/tag/v1.8.0

## Long version — `#dayz-inventory` / `#general`

> ## DayZ Inventory 1.8.0
>
> **From 1.20.1 to 26.3, on all three loaders, from one codebase.**
>
> The mod itself has not changed. What changed is how many versions you can play it on — and two
> bugs that were hiding on the older ones.
>
> ### What is supported now
>
> | Minecraft | Fabric | NeoForge | Forge |
> | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> | 1.20.1 – 1.20.4 | ✅ | — | — |
> | 1.20.5 | ✅ | — | — |
> | 1.20.6 – 1.21.1 | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
> | 1.21.2 | ✅ | ✅ | — |
> | 1.21.3 – 1.21.11 | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
> | 26.1 – 26.3 | ✅ | ✅ | — |
>
> That is **23 Minecraft versions and 53 files**, all built from a single branch. Each download is
> named with its Minecraft version and loader, so pick the one that matches both.
>
> ### Fixed: chests and barrels on 1.20.1–1.20.4
>
> The hooks that route a chest or barrel into the DayZ container view were aimed at the wrong
> method on those versions. The result was not a crash — chests and barrels simply opened their
> normal vanilla screens, so it looked like a missing feature rather than a bug. They open properly
> now.
>
> ### Fixed: the JEI / REI / EMI and Curios buttons
>
> These could be drawn without doing anything when clicked. Three separate causes: the
> recipe-viewer button assumed `O` instead of asking the installed viewer what its toggle key
> actually is; the simulated keypress lost whether the binding was a key or a mouse button; and
> reading a keybind looked at the wrong field. Curios is also a NeoForge/Forge mod, so on Fabric
> there is nothing to open — that button now opens the vanilla inventory rather than pretending.
>
> ### Forge went from 1.21.5 to 1.21.11
>
> Forge changed its event bus in 1.21.6, and that is what had been holding the newer Forge builds
> back. That is done, so Forge now ships for 1.20.6 through 1.21.11.
>
> ### The gaps, honestly
>
> NeoForge on 1.20.2–1.20.4 is not happening: 1.20.2 still used an older networking API, 1.20.3
> never got a NeoForge release at all, and 1.20.4's API predates the payload system the mod uses.
> Forge 1.20.1 needs a reobfuscation step today's toolchain cannot do. Forge also skipped 1.21.2
> entirely, and the 26.x line is NeoForge-only. Fabric covers every version regardless.
>
