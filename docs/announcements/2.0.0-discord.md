## Long version — `#dayz-inventory` / `#general`

> ## DayZ Inventory 2.0.0
>
> The biggest update since the mod went multi-version, and most of it comes straight from your
> comments.
>
> ### Forge 1.20.1 is back
>
> The last Forge 1.20.1 file was 1.4.3. 1.20.1 Forge is now built from the same sources as every
> other version, so it gets everything below. Any Forge 47.x works.
>
> ### The buttons work
>
> - **CURIOS** opens the Curios screen instead of doing nothing or closing the inventory.
> - **JEI / REI / EMI** shows and hides the item list.
> - **ACCESSORIES** is new, next to CURIOS and TRINKETS.
>
> ### New
>
> - **Config file** — `config/dayz_inventory.properties`: walk-over pickup, chest handling, Vicinity
>   radius (1–8 blocks), the inventory key, and the new panels.
> - **Status effects** on the Survivor panel, with name and time left on hover.
> - **Health, food and thirst** bars. Thirst works with Tough As Nails, Thirst Was Taken and
>   Dehydration.
> - **SORT** buttons for your inventory and for open chests, barrels and shulker boxes.
> - **Shift-click** now moves items between inventory and hotbar.
> - **Mouse Tweaks** works in the DayZ screen.
> - Chinese, French, Japanese and Korean translations.
>
> ### Fixed
>
> Shulker boxes can no longer go inside shulker boxes, furnaces only take what they should, locked
> containers stay locked, Curse of Binding sticks, quick pickup into a full inventory no longer
> deletes items, creative players can pick things up again, and the Vicinity panel is no longer cut
> off on small windows. The full list is in the changelog.
>
> ### Still to come
>
> **Item sizes** (Petite Inventory compatibility or a built-in grid) are planned for 2.1.
>
