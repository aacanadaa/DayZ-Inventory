## Long version — `#dayz-inventory` / `#general`

> ## DayZ Inventory 1.8.2
>
> **1.8.0 shipped broken on Fabric. This release fixes that and three more.**
>
> The mod itself has not changed. What changed is that the files load and draw the way they should.
>
> ### What was wrong
>
> **Fabric 1.8.0 would not start at all.** Two separate causes: `fabric.mod.json` wrote its Minecraft
> and Java requirements in a syntax Fabric's version parser cannot read, and the client entrypoint
> class had never been compiled into the jar. All 23 Fabric files refused to load. The metadata was
> fixed in 1.8.1; the mixin refmap reference that Fabric below 26.1 then still needed is fixed in
> 1.8.2.
>
> **1.21.6–1.21.10 crashed at launch on Fabric, NeoForge *and* Forge.** The mixin that makes the Hands
> panel count as a hovered slot was aimed at a method that moved in 1.21.6. A mixin that finds no
> target is a hard crash, not a silent no-op. Fixed.
>
> **1.21.6–1.21.11 drew the inventory at the wrong scale.** The panels came out larger than the screen
> with a gray dim over them, and the Survivor player preview was clipped by its own scissor box. If
> you saw a screenshot of a giant, dimmed inventory, that was this. Both fixed.
>
